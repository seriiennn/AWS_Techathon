# VisionWell product steering

VisionWell Agent is a browser-local health-education and experimental vision-task prototype for adult university screen users. It supports SDG 3 but is not a clinical diagnostic device. Use “experimental binocular task performance,” “Estimated Distance,” and “general eye-care suggestion”; never infer refractive error, disease, colour-vision diagnosis, causality from screen time, or treatment benefit. Keep camera frames and original screenshots local and non-persistent. Always retain a complete manual demonstration path.
